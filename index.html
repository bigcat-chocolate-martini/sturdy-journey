"Pushed Beyond 180-Day Window" reflects opportunities that are still open and being worked, but whose close date moved out far enough that they no longer fall within the report's 180-day view; these are not lost or removed, just temporarily out of scope, and any accompanying stage change is shown so a backward slip isn't missed. Both snapshots in this comparison are full unfiltered pipeline exports, so pushed-out and removed deals are distinguished reliably.
