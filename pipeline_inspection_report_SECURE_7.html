This summary compares the pipeline export from July 27, 2026 against August 4, 2026. "Confirmed Closed Won" reflects opportunities with a Stage of Sales Complete or Closed Won in the latest export. "Sales Ops Cleanup Effort" reflects opportunities that were open in the prior export and were deliberately removed from pipeline as part of a Sales Ops effort to clear out stale and outdated opportunities — this is a data hygiene improvement, not a loss. "Pushed Beyond 180-Day Window" reflects opportunities that are still open and being worked, but whose close date moved out far enough that they no longer fall within the report's 180-day view; these are not lost or removed, just temporarily out of scope, and any accompanying stage change is shown so a backward slip isn't missed. Deal counts in the main pipeline view reflect open opportunities closing within 180 days at the time of each report. Both snapshots in this comparison are full unfiltered pipeline exports, so pushed-out and removed deals are distinguished reliably.
